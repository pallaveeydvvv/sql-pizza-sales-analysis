# Pizza Sales Analysis Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pizza sales data using SQL to extract meaningful business insights related to revenue, customer behavior, delivery performance, and product trends.

The analysis was performed using SQL concepts such as joins, views, aggregations, window functions, CTEs, and subqueries.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze customer ordering behavior
* Identify top-performing pizzas
* Calculate total and category-wise revenue
* Track successful and failed deliveries
* Discover high revenue generating products
* Measure contribution of top pizzas to overall revenue

---

## Database Schema

The project contains the following tables:

* customers
* orders
* order_details
* pizzas
* pizza_types

Relationships between tables were modeled using primary and foreign keys.

---

* Joins

# Business Insights

## Revenue & Delivery Insights

1. Only successful and delivered orders were considered for revenue analysis to ensure accurate business performance measurement.
2. A significant portion of total revenue is generated by the top 5 pizzas, indicating that a small set of products contribute heavily to overall sales.
3. High-performing pizzas can be prioritized in promotions, advertisements, and combo offers to maximize revenue generation.
4. Failed and cancelled orders directly reduce potential revenue and should be monitored to improve operational efficiency.

---
## Customer & Ordering Insights

5. Some customers place orders significantly more frequently than others, indicating the presence of repeat customers and loyal buyers.
6. Successful delivery counts are higher for certain customers, suggesting consistent purchasing behavior.
7. Customer ordering patterns can help identify premium or high-value customers for loyalty programs and targeted marketing.

---

## Product Insights
8. Certain pizzas are ordered more frequently than others, showing strong customer preference trends.
9. The pizzas with the highest order quantity are not always the highest revenue generators, indicating that pricing also plays a major role in profitability.
10. Premium-priced pizzas contribute strongly to revenue even with comparatively fewer orders.

---

## Operational Insights
11. Order volume varies across different dates, indicating peak business days and potential high-demand periods.
12. Tracking daily successful deliveries can help optimize staffing, inventory planning, and delivery operations.
13. Understanding peak ordering periods can improve resource allocation and customer service efficiency.

---

## Analytical Insights
14. Window functions such as DENSE_RANK() were used to identify top-performing pizzas based on revenue contribution.
15. CTEs and Views were utilized to create modular and reusable analytical workflows for cleaner and more maintainable SQL analysis.
