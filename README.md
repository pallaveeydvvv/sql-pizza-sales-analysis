# SQL Pizza Sales Analysis 🍕

This project is based on analyzing pizza sales data using SQL to understand customer behavior, delivery trends, product performance, and revenue generation.

I built this project while learning SQL and database concepts to improve my analytical thinking, query writing, and understanding of relational databases.

---

# Project Objective & Business Understanding

The main purpose of this project was to understand how raw transactional data can be transformed into meaningful business insights using SQL.

Instead of only writing queries, the focus was on analyzing customer behavior, product performance, delivery trends, and revenue generation patterns from the available raw data.

Through this analysis, multiple business-related questions were explored, such as:

* Which pizzas generate the highest revenue?
* Which products are ordered most frequently?
* Which customers place the most orders?
* How do successful and failed deliveries affect revenue?
* Which days experience higher order volumes?
* How can delivery and sales performance be evaluated using SQL?

The project demonstrates how SQL can be used not only for database querying but also for business decision-making and operational analysis.

---

# What Was Analyzed From the Raw Data

The raw dataset contained customer, order, pizza, and delivery-related information spread across multiple tables.

Using SQL, the following areas were analyzed:

## Customer Analysis

* Customer ordering frequency
* Repeat customer behavior
* Successful vs failed customer orders

## Order Analysis

* Daily order trends
* Order status distribution
* Successful and cancelled deliveries

## Product Analysis

* Most ordered pizzas
* Highest quantity pizzas sold
* Highest revenue-generating pizzas
* Pizza pricing impact on profitability

## Revenue Analysis

* Revenue generated from successful deliveries
* Revenue contribution of top-performing pizzas
* Product-wise revenue breakdown

# How These Insights Can Help a Business

The insights generated from this project can help businesses make better operational and strategic decisions.

## Revenue Optimization

Identifying high revenue-generating pizzas helps businesses focus on products that contribute most to profitability. These products can be prioritized in promotions, advertisements, and combo offers.

---

## Inventory & Demand Planning

Understanding which pizzas are ordered more frequently helps businesses manage inventory more efficiently and reduce stock-related issues during peak demand periods.

---

## Customer Retention

Analyzing repeat customers helps identify loyal customers and supports the creation of loyalty programs, discounts, and personalized marketing strategies.

---

## Operational Efficiency

Analyzing delivery success and failed orders helps businesses identify operational inefficiencies and improve delivery performance.

---

## Peak Demand Management

Daily order trend analysis helps businesses identify peak ordering days, which can improve staffing, inventory preparation, and delivery management.

---

## Business Decision-Making

Overall, the project demonstrates how raw relational data can be converted into actionable business insights using SQL-based analytical techniques.


# Business Insights

## Revenue Insights

* A small group of pizzas contributes significantly to overall revenue.
* Revenue analysis shows that successful deliveries are the primary revenue source.
* High-priced pizzas generate strong revenue even with comparatively fewer orders.

---

## Customer Insights

* Some customers consistently place more orders than others.
* Repeat customers indicate customer retention and loyalty patterns.
* Delivery success trends help identify customer ordering behavior.

---

## Product Insights

* Certain pizzas are consistently ordered more frequently.
* Top-selling pizzas are not always the highest revenue-generating products.
* Quantity sold and pricing both influence profitability.

---

## Operational Insights

* Order volumes vary significantly across different dates.
* Peak delivery days can help optimize staffing and operations.
* Failed and cancelled orders directly impact revenue generation.
