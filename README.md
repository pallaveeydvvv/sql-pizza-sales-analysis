# SQL Pizza Sales Analysis 🍕

## Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data using SQL to extract business insights related to customer behavior, product performance, order trends, and revenue generation.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful insights using SQL queries, joins, aggregations, views, and analytical techniques.

## Key Analysis Performed

### Customer Analysis

* Top customers by total orders
* Repeat customer behavior
* Successful vs failed customer orders

### Order Analysis

* Daily order trends
* Order status distribution
* Successful and cancelled deliveries

### Product Analysis

* Most ordered pizzas
* Highest quantity pizzas sold
* Highest revenue-generating pizzas
* Pizza pricing impact on revenue

### Revenue Analysis

* Revenue from successful deliveries
* Product-wise revenue contribution
* Top revenue-generating pizzas

## Business Insights

* A small group of pizzas contributes a major portion of total revenue.
* Top-selling pizzas are not always the highest revenue-generating pizzas.
* Successful deliveries drive overall business revenue.
* Order volume varies significantly across different days.
* Repeat customers contribute consistently to sales growth.

## Business Decisions & Recommendations

* Promote top revenue-generating pizzas through advertisements and featured listings.
* Create combo offers around popular pizzas to increase average order value.
* Offer discounts on low-demand days to boost sales.
* Introduce loyalty rewards for repeat customers.
* Reduce failed and cancelled orders to improve revenue performance.
